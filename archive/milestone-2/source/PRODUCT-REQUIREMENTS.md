## External prerequisites

The operator must provide a supported Linux Docker/Compose host, pinned image digests, trust roots, scoped workload identities, provider enforcement and a complete credential/route inventory. Companion credentials and the Zanii write identity stay outside the agent. No assistant-provider environment credential is reused. Live SaaS/ledger access also requires an accepted egress profile; the supplied backend-only network intentionally cannot reach arbitrary external services.

External acceptance is open. A working local implementation or a completed draft paper does not close it.
